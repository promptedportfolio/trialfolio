### What R05-T01 found

On 2026-11-01, R05-T01 checked, offline, with no request and no credit, every saved screen-backtest response in the main checkout's `reference/`, and the committed synthetic ones, with a script committed beside its [run record](../../reference/return-series/README.md). The owner's 77 saved files hold 74 distinct responses, from 0.1.0's reference call to 0.4.0's diagnostic: 3 that rebalance every week, with 523 periods each, and 71 every 4 weeks, with 131, ranked by formulas, by saved ranking systems, and by definitions sent with `rank_update`.

- **Each holds the series as the layout records them.** Every `chart` has its five arrays parallel, with one entry for each of the 2,609 weekdays from 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31, the requested dates, and both levels start at 100. Every period's `Ret%` and `Bench%` reproduces from the levels at its `Tran Dt` and `End Dt`, with either rebalancing, and with a definition. `total_return` is the last level minus 100 in all 74, and `annualized_return`, `max_drawdown`, and `standard_deviation` reproduce from the levels, at the precision or within what the levels' rounding allows.
- **What 0.5.0 can compute.** So each saved run, and each case of a saved experiment with a response, can get every proposed analysis of its daily levels and of its periods. Three attempts, the 0.3.0 walk-through's, have no response. No saved run or experiment declares split dates, so their period splits are unavailable. This resolves the [open question](#open-questions) as its default gives it.
- **The rolling windows' bound holds.** In every saved `chart`, no window of 7, 30, 90, 180, or 365 days, by the outline's rule, is more than 2 days longer than its length.
- **Cases align by date.** Every case of each saved experiment has the same `chart` dates, as the [open question](#open-questions) on aligning cases assumes.
- **A market closure shows as a repeated level in the entry after it.** Each closure has an entry, whose levels move from the entry before, apart from the first closure, 2016-01-01, which starts every `chart`, and one of the benchmark's, in the response with IWM. The next weekday's entry repeats the closure's: the benchmark's always, and the strategy's unless a period's `Tran Dt` is the closure. Rebalance dates fall on closures, and no `chart` ends on one. At least two readings of a level's date fit this, and Portfolio123 documents none: a new [open question](#open-questions).
- **Reported values agree with the saved series, but often only within its rounding.** `Excess%` differs from `Ret%` minus `Bench%`, by their rounding, in 2,804 of 10,870 periods, so a series table that calculated it again would change it, as [D-47](../spec.md#decisions) says it mustn't. And calculated from the levels, the strategy's maximum drawdown differs from Portfolio123's beyond its reported digits, read at the precision, in 26 of the 74 responses, and its annualized return in 1 more, each within what the levels' rounding allows. Read that way, [required behavior](#required-behavior) 5 would flag those 27: a new [open question](#open-questions), for the owner, since it bears on a settled part.
- **R01-T02's record of the reference response** gives its `100 USD` columns as equal to the `chart` levels in all 131 periods. Compared exactly, 5 of the benchmark's are one unit of the fourth decimal apart. R05-T01 added a note to [that record](../../reference/p123api-screen-backtest/README.md#checks-for-r01-t02). The layout that record backs, in [contracts.md](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1), still calls the two columns reproduced, without saying how closely, and in the 74 responses, 328 of the strategy's 10,870 values and 232 of the benchmark's are one unit apart from the level ([run record](../../reference/return-series/README.md#the-checks)). R05-T02, which specifies the series table in contracts.md, carries that observation into the layout's row for them.

These findings, the open question they resolve, and the note on R01-T02's record await the owner's confirmation. The run record also gives what wasn't checked, such as runs and experiments outside `reference/`, and other dates and settings, since every saved request asked for the same dates at the same precision.

## User outcome

Read, in the report of a run or an experiment, new or saved, each case's dated returns, a fixed set of descriptive analyses of them, each case's comparison with the baseline, and its equity and drawdown charts, without assembling a spreadsheet ([OUT-02 and OUT-03](../spec.md#users-and-outcomes)). This is the roadmap's example scenario, read in the CLI's report ([D-42](../spec.md#decisions), [release planning requirements](../roadmap.md#release-planning-requirements)).

**The research task.** Reproducing published factor research describes each variant's returns, not only its summary statistics: rolling returns over several holding periods, drawdowns, and the measures over the periods before and after the paper's sample and its publication ([primary use](../spec.md#primary-use), [METH-09.7, METH-09.9, and METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)). The researcher D-42 names needs each case's dated returns first, since every later statistic reads them: the roadmap's [statistical evaluation](../roadmap.md#after-030) target, 0.6.0, infers from the same series.

**The manual work it removes.** Today a report shows Portfolio123's summary statistics, and an experiment's report shows its cases' metrics side by side, without differences. The saved response holds each period's returns and each weekday's value of 100 invested, but Trial Folio doesn't interpret them ([METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)). A researcher who wants a drawdown's dates, rolling returns, period splits, a case's differences from the baseline, or a chart reads the responses into a spreadsheet, calculates each one, draws the charts, and records by hand which method they used, for every case of every experiment. With 0.5.0, the report gives them, by one fixed, versioned method, and so does a saved run's or experiment's report rendered again, offline, from its records.

## Included scope

- **Each case's dated series, as a normalized table.** The daily levels of 100 invested, for the strategy and the benchmark, from the response's `chart`; and each rebalance period's returns, for the strategy, the benchmark, and their difference, from its `results.rows` ([the verified layout](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1)). Each value keeps its date, its source location, its provenance, and the digits the response gave it. For a run, and for each case of an experiment, as it's normalized.
- **A fixed, versioned analysis set.** Its first version, analysis set 1.0.0, is calculated from those series alone, identically for every case and every saved run or experiment ([METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)):
  - full-period summaries, the descriptive t-value of mean excess return per rebalance period among them, labeled descriptive with its number of periods ([METH-04.6](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge));
  - drawdowns, from the daily levels;
  - rolling returns over several holding periods ([METH-09.7](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research));
  - period splits, at dates the configuration declares before the run ([METH-09.9](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)).

  Where it recalculates a metric Portfolio123 reports, such as total return or maximum drawdown, the report shows both. R05-T02 specifies every calculation ([open questions](#open-questions)).
- **The explicit baseline comparison,** in an experiment's report: each case beside the baseline, with the difference of each metric and each analysis value ([D-42](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **Equity and drawdown charts,** drawn from the daily levels, as inline SVG in the self-contained report, which stays free of scripts ([REQ-08](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [D-42](../spec.md#decisions)): each case's, with its benchmark, and in an experiment the baseline's beside it.
- **Saved runs and experiments, rendered again.** `trialfolio report` renders a saved experiment's report again, offline, as it already does a saved run's, so the runs and experiments that releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote get the analytics from their saved responses, without a request ([INV-05](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)). It writes nothing into them ([ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).

## Explicit exclusions

- **Inference.** Confidence intervals, p-values, significance tests and grades, multiple-testing adjustment, the bootstrap, the deflated Sharpe ratio, and the probability of backtest overfitting are the [statistical evaluation](../roadmap.md#after-030) target's, after its methods ADR ([METH-04](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge)). The t-value is descriptive, and is never called a test.
- **Measures that need a risk-free series,** such as a Sharpe or Sortino ratio Trial Folio calculates, since no response holds one ([METH-01.3](../methodology.md#meth-01-define-the-question-before-measuring-success)). The report still shows Portfolio123's own, as reported.
- **Analyses that need new provider data:** repeated backtests from other entry dates, rolling screens, quantile buckets, and simulations ([METH-09.7 and METH-09.8](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research), [descriptive analysis before further acquisition](../roadmap.md#descriptive-analysis-before-further-acquisition)). Gross versus net results, and cost sensitivity, which need verified cost semantics, stay in the reproduction and sensitivity workflow ([METH-06.6](../methodology.md#meth-06-robustness-and-economic-usefulness)).
- **Ordering, ranking, or selecting cases by a value,** a "best" case, and any aggregate score ([METH-07](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
- **A review's report,** which keeps 0.4.0's: an experiment's report gives the comparison with the baseline. Comparing separate runs by their series, such as the same screen on two universes ([D-19](../spec.md#decisions)), and comparing the cases of two experiments, wait for a later release.
- **A graphical application, interactive charts, and any script,** which stay in the results workspace ([REQ-08](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [D-42](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **Study records:** protocols, deviation records, and amendments, which 0.6.0 and the reproduction and sensitivity workflow define.
- **Any change to what Portfolio123 reported.** `metrics.csv` and its values stay as 0.1.0 reads them.
- **`trialfolio report` writing into what it reads.** It writes only into its own output directory, and the saved run or experiment keeps its files ([ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).
- **Ranking snapshots,** which hold no series.
- **Parallel or scheduled execution,** as in 0.4.0.

## Supported inputs and examples

Every run and experiment that 0.4.0 accepts, and every saved run and experiment that releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote. A configuration may also declare the dates of period splits. The intended shape, which R05-T02 specifies and may change:

```yaml
kind: experiment
# ...the experiment's other keys, as in 0.4.0
analysis:
  period_splits:
    - date: '2012-12-31'
      label: 'End of the paper''s sample'
    - date: '2016-06-30'
      label: 'Publication'
```

And the commands:

```sh
trialfolio run value-study.yaml --out experiments/value-study/
trialfolio report experiments/value-study/ --out reports/value-study/
```

The first writes each case's series and analyses with its normalized tables, and draws them into the session's report. The second renders a saved experiment's report again, offline, into a new directory.

## Required behavior

1. **Only what the response holds.** Every series comes from a case's saved response. A case whose response lacks one says so, with the reason, and nothing is reconstructed from summary statistics, filled with zero, or charted without its series ([METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim), [INV-03](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)).
2. **Offline and repeatable.** The analyses and charts come from archived artifacts, with no provider request. The same records, rendered with the same version, give the same bytes. `trialfolio report` writes nothing into the run or experiment it reads, and no command changes a file a run or experiment already holds ([INV-05](../spec.md#enduring-invariants), [ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).
3. **One fixed method.** The analysis set's version is recorded with every value it gives, and applies identically to every case and every saved run or experiment. Changing a calculation makes a new version ([METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)).
4. **Calculated values say how.** Each is labeled calculated, with its method, the analysis set's version, its inputs, and the Trial Folio version, and is never given more digits than its inputs justify ([INV-06](../spec.md#enduring-invariants), [METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)).
5. **Reported values stay as reported.** Where the analysis set recalculates a metric Portfolio123 reported, the report shows both, and flags a difference beyond the reported digits rather than choosing one ([INV-08](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)).
6. **Descriptive, and labeled so.** Each summary of overlapping windows says they aren't independent, with its number of windows and of non-overlapping windows. The t-value is labeled descriptive, with its number of periods. Statistical validation and trading readiness read "Not assessed" ([METH-04.6](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge), [METH-09.7](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research), [METH-07.3](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
7. **A neutral comparison.** The baseline comparison and the charts order, rank, and mark no case by a value, and name no case best ([REQ-13](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [METH-07](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
8. **Everything 0.4.0 requires still holds,** and every artifact releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote stays readable ([INV-12](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)).

## Failure and incomplete-data behavior

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| A case's response has no `chart`, or its arrays aren't parallel, dated, or numbers | That case's daily series is unavailable, with the reason. Its drawdowns, rolling returns, and charts are unavailable, and the other cases are still analyzed. |
| A case's `chart` isn't one entry per weekday from its start date to its end date, as the [verified layout](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1) records it | That case's daily series is unavailable, with the reason, as for a `chart` that can't be read. Its drawdowns, rolling returns, and charts are unavailable. Its period series is still analyzed, and so are the other cases. |
| A case's response has no period rows, or a row's return isn't a number | That case's period series, or that row's value, is unavailable with its reason, never zero. Each analysis that needs it is unavailable with `input_unavailable`. |
| Too few observations for a window or a statistic | That value is unavailable, with the count it had, and is never calculated from fewer |
| A recalculated value differs from Portfolio123's beyond the reported digits | Both are shown, and the difference is flagged |
| A declared split date is outside a case's coverage | That split is flagged, and its values are unavailable where the coverage doesn't reach. No window is shortened to fit ([METH-05.4](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)). |
| A case's coverage differs from the baseline's | The comparison names the difference, and labels each value calculated over different dates |
| `trialfolio report` is given a directory that isn't a complete run or experiment | It fails, as for a run, with the error R05-T03 specifies, and writes nothing |
| An artifact has a schema version with no reader | `artifact.unknown_schema_version`, exit 3, as in 0.4.0 |

0.4.0's, with each case's series table and analysis set's table among its normalized tables, as R05-T03 specifies, and in its report, the analyses, the baseline comparison, and the charts. `trialfolio report`'s output for a saved experiment.

## Acceptance criteria

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| R05-AC01 | For a run, and for each case of an experiment, the series table holds every daily level its saved response holds, for the strategy and the benchmark, and every period's return it holds, for the strategy, the benchmark, and their difference, each with its date, its source location, its provenance, and the response's digits |
| R05-AC02 | A case whose response has no usable daily levels, or no period rows, says which is unavailable and why, in its table and its report; each analysis that needs it is unavailable with `input_unavailable`; a case without usable daily levels has no chart, while a case without period rows still has its charts and the analyses of its daily levels; and the other cases are still analyzed |

| R05-AC04 | Where the analysis set recalculates a metric Portfolio123 reported, the report shows both, and a fixture whose two values differ beyond the reported digits is flagged, with neither replacing the other |
| R05-AC05 | An experiment's report shows each case beside the baseline, with the difference of each metric and each analysis value, with its unit and digits, or the reason it isn't differenced; and it orders, ranks, and marks no case by a value, and names no case best |
| R05-AC06 | The report draws each case's equity and drawdown charts from its daily levels, with its benchmark, and in an experiment the baseline, as inline SVG; it holds no script, event-handler attribute, or external resource; and a case without usable daily levels has no chart, and the report says why |
| R05-AC07 | Each summary of overlapping windows says they aren't independent, with its number of windows and of non-overlapping windows; the t-value is labeled descriptive, with its number of periods, and is never called a test; and statistical validation and trading readiness read "Not assessed" |
| R05-AC08 | When the configuration declares split dates, the report gives each split's values, with its dates and its share of the coverage; a run or experiment that declares none says period splits are unavailable because no dates were declared; and a date outside a case's coverage is flagged, with no window shortened to fit |
| R05-AC09 | `trialfolio report` renders a saved run's and a saved experiment's report again, offline: from the committed historical fixtures, `tests/fixtures/runs/synthetic-run-1.0.0/`, `tests/fixtures/runs/synthetic-run-1.1.0/`, `tests/fixtures/experiments/synthetic-experiment-1.0.0/`, and `tests/fixtures/experiments/synthetic-experiment-1.1.0/`, with each analysis of the daily series unavailable, with the reason, since their `chart` isn't one entry per weekday; and with the analytics, from a new synthetic fixture whose `chart` has one entry per weekday. It opens no network connection, and writes nothing in the fixtures' directories; the same input, rendered with the same version, gives the same bytes |
| R05-AC10 | Every artifact releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote stays readable, and every schema `v0.4.0` committed keeps its bytes |
