### What R05-T01 found

On 2026-11-01, R05-T01 checked, offline, with no request and no credit, every saved screen-backtest response in the main checkout's `reference/`, and the committed synthetic ones, with a script committed beside its [run record](../../reference/return-series/README.md). The owner's 77 saved files hold 74 distinct responses, from 0.1.0's reference call to 0.4.0's diagnostic: 3 that rebalance every week, with 523 periods each, and 71 every 4 weeks, with 131, ranked by formulas, by saved ranking systems, and by definitions sent with `rank_update`.

- **Each holds the series as the layout records them.** Every `chart` has its five arrays parallel, with one entry for each of the 2,609 weekdays from 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31, the requested dates, and both levels start at 100. Every period's `Ret%` and `Bench%` reproduces from the levels at its `Tran Dt` and `End Dt`, with either rebalancing, and with a definition. `total_return` is the last level minus 100 in all 74, and `annualized_return`, `max_drawdown`, and `standard_deviation` reproduce from the levels, at the precision or within what the levels' rounding allows.
- **What 0.5.0 can compute.** So each saved run, and each case of a saved experiment with a response, can get every proposed analysis of its daily levels and of its periods. Three attempts, the 0.3.0 walk-through's, have no response. No saved run or experiment declares split dates, so their period splits are unavailable. This resolves the [open question](#open-questions) as its default gives it.
- **The rolling windows' bound holds.** In every saved `chart`, no window of 7, 30, 90, 180, or 365 days, by the outline's rule, is more than 2 days longer than its length.
- **Cases align by date.** Every case of each saved experiment has the same `chart` dates, as the [open question](#open-questions) on aligning cases assumes.
- **A market closure shows as a repeated level in the entry after it.** Each closure has an entry, whose levels move from the entry before, apart from the first closure, 2016-01-01, which starts every `chart`, and one of the benchmark's, in the response with IWM. The next weekday's entry repeats the closure's: the benchmark's always, and the strategy's unless a period's `Tran Dt` is the closure. Rebalance dates fall on closures, and no `chart` ends on one. At least two readings of a level's date fit this, and Portfolio123 documents none: a new [open question](#open-questions).
- **Reported values agree with the saved series, but often only within its rounding.** `Excess%` differs from `Ret%` minus `Bench%`, by their rounding, in 2,804 of 10,870 periods, so a series table that calculated it again would change it, as [D-47](../spec.md#decisions) says it mustn't. And calculated from the levels, the strategy's maximum drawdown differs from Portfolio123's beyond its reported digits, read at the precision, in 26 of the 74 responses, and its annualized return in 1 more, each within what the levels' rounding allows. Read that way, [required behavior](#required-behavior) 5 would flag those 27: a new [open question](#open-questions), which the owner decided on 2026-11-01, so that a difference within that rounding isn't flagged ([D-48](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **R01-T02's record of the reference response** gives its `100 USD` columns as equal to the `chart` levels in all 131 periods. Compared exactly, 5 of the benchmark's are one unit of the fourth decimal apart. R05-T01 added a note to [that record](../../reference/p123api-screen-backtest/README.md#checks-for-r01-t02). The layout that record backs, in [contracts.md](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1), still calls the two columns reproduced, without saying how closely, and in the 74 responses, 328 of the strategy's 10,870 values and 232 of the benchmark's are one unit apart from the level ([run record](../../reference/return-series/README.md#the-checks)). R05-T02, which specifies the series table in contracts.md, carries that observation into the layout's row for them, as the owner confirmed on 2026-11-01.

The owner confirmed these findings, the open question they resolve, and the note on R01-T02's record on 2026-11-01. **Details confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-02,** each with the alternative where there was one:

1. **Two tables, one row per value.** `series.csv` has a row for each daily level and each period return, with its date, source location, provenance, and digits ([R05-AC01](#acceptance-criteria)); `analysis.csv` a row for each value of the analysis set, with its method, the set's version, its inputs, and the Trial Folio version ([required behavior](#required-behavior) 4). The alternative, a column for each subject, would leave no place for each value's own availability and source.
2. **The daily series is read only in the verified form:** `chart`'s three arrays Trial Folio reads, the same length; dates increasing; levels above 0, both starting at 100; and the dates exactly the weekdays from the case's `start_date` to its `end_date`. Otherwise its two daily rows are unavailable, with `blank_in_source`, `unparseable_in_source`, or `not_daily` ([D-47](../spec.md#decisions)). The start at 100 is part of the check, since a level is a value of 100 invested only then. The alternative, levels read relative to their first, would analyze a form no saved response has.
3. **New units and codes, in the new tables only:** the unit `index`, for a level, and `pp`, for a difference of two percents in `analysis.csv`; and the reason codes `not_daily`, `too_few_observations`, `too_short`, `outside_coverage`, and `not_recovered`. `metrics.csv`, `settings.csv`, and `differences.csv` keep their units and codes, and their schemas their bytes ([R05-AC10](#acceptance-criteria)).
4. **Period rows earliest first,** though `results.rows` gives them newest first; the daily rows keep `chart`'s order.
5. **Digits.** Every value in percent, percentage points, or a ratio is written with the request's `precision`, 4, the digits its inputs have, rounded once, half to even, after a calculation in `decimal` with 50 significant digits; counts and days are whole; and a value that rounds to zero is written without a sign, as a difference is: `0.0000`, never `-0.0000`. The alternative, fewer digits for some values, such as a share, would choose digits no input gives; and keeping a zero's sign, as `decimal` does, would write two values that are equal at their digits in two ways.
6. **The analyses** are those the [open question](#open-questions)'s default lists, with three additions the criteria need: `period_count`, and, for split segments, `coverage_share`, the share of the coverage R05-AC08 gives, and `spanning_period_count`. Mean, median, best, and worst periods are given for the strategy, the benchmark, and the excess.
7. **The tracking error and the information ratio are per rebalance period,** not annualized. The alternative, annualizing by the square root of the periods in a year, needs a count of periods a year that Portfolio123 doesn't document for its rebalancing, whose first and last periods can be short.
8. **No annualized return over less than a year,** `too_short`, so a split segment of two months isn't stated at an annual rate it never held. The alternative, annualizing any span, as Portfolio123's own `annualized_return` may, would show such a segment at that rate.
9. **Minimums are what each definition needs:** 1 period or window for a mean, median, percentile, best, worst, or share; 2 periods for a standard deviation and what divides by it; 2 levels for a return. The alternative, larger minimums, such as 2 non-overlapping windows for a rolling summary, would choose a threshold no source gives; each summary gives its counts instead ([required behavior](#required-behavior) 6).
10. **No period is left out.** A value of a subject over a segment needs every one of its periods to have that subject's return, or it's unavailable with `input_unavailable` (METH-05.4).
11. **Drawdowns.** A drawdown's peak is the first date the level reached it, and its trough the first date of the largest decline, as R05-T01's script found them; its recovery is the first later date at or above the peak, or `not_recovered`; and the longest time below a peak is in calendar days, from the date before the level falls below its running peak, the last at that peak, to the first date it's back at it, flagged `not_recovered` when it runs to the segment's end. The alternative, starting a time below a peak on the first date the level reached that peak, as the maximum drawdown's peak is dated, would count an earlier time below the same peak, which had recovered, in a later one.
12. **Percentiles** are Hyndman and Fan's definition 7, linear interpolation between the sorted returns, which `numpy.percentile` and `statistics.quantiles` with `method="inclusive"` compute, so a reference calculation is at hand. With one window, both percentiles are its return, as definition 7 gives them; Python 3.12's `quantiles` raises an error there, unlike 3.14's, so a reference calculation on 3.12 needs another way for that case. The alternatives, such as the nearest rank, give other values for few windows.
13. **Non-overlapping windows** are counted from the earliest: the most windows no two of which overlap, where a window that starts on the date another ends doesn't overlap it.
14. **Beating, and a positive excess, are strict,** and the windows' returns are compared before rounding: a tie isn't beating, and an excess of 0 isn't positive.
15. **Period splits:** an optional `analysis` key of screen and experiment configurations 1.2.0, with `period_splits`: 1 to 10 splits, each a `date` and a `label` of 1 to 200 characters, in date order, with distinct labels, never sent, and outside every `case_id`. A date outside the requested dates is accepted, since a paper's sample may end before the backtest starts, and flagged when the analyses are calculated. The bound of 10 keeps the report's tables finite; the alternative was no bound.
16. **Plans 1.4.0 to 1.7.0** record the splits, one for each of plans 1.0.0 to 1.3.0, so a plan without splits keeps its 0.4.0 version and shape. The alternative, an `analysis` field in every plan 0.5.0 builds, would give every new plan a new version, with splits or without.
17. **Segments.** `split-0` and the last split segment hold what the coverage holds on their side of their declared date, while a segment between two declared dates is unavailable, with `outside_coverage`, unless the coverage reaches both, so it's never shortened to fit ([R05-AC08](#acceptance-criteria)). A segment's levels run from the latest `chart` date on or before its first date to the latest on or before its last, so the segments' total returns compound to the whole. The coverage the splits divide is the result's `coverage_start` and `coverage_end`, or, when either is unavailable, as without period rows, its first and last `chart` dates, so a result without period rows keeps its split segments' analyses of its daily levels ([R05-AC02](#acceptance-criteria)). Its split segments' values from the periods, `period_count` and `spanning_period_count` included, are then unavailable, with `input_unavailable`: the coverage is unavailable only when the result has no period, a period's date can't be read, or the dates contradict each other, so the segments' periods aren't known, and none is calculated without a period it may hold. The alternative, placing a period with one unreadable date by its other, where that date alone decides, would keep a few more segments' values, with a rule for each side of each declared date. A declared date before the coverage's first date, or after its last, is flagged. One on the first or last date is within the coverage, and isn't flagged: the segment on its outer side is that one date, and its values are unavailable as in any segment too short for them.
18. **A period that spans a split date is in neither segment,** and each counts it in `spanning_period_count`. The alternative, crediting it to the segment that holds its `End Dt`, would credit part of a return to a side it wasn't earned on.
19. **Split segments get the period-return and daily-level analyses,** the full-period summaries of METH-09.9, but not the rolling returns.
20. **Reconciliation** compares `total_return`, `annualized_return`, and `max_drawdown`, for the strategy and the benchmark, over the `full` segment, with the ranges R05-T01's script computes, which [D-48](../spec.md#decisions) gives, writing `agrees`, `differs`, or `not_compared`. The standard deviation isn't in the set, so it isn't reconciled.
21. **The comparison with the baseline** pairs the metrics by the rules of a review's metric rows, and the analysis values by segment, analysis, subject, and window. A value from period returns isn't differenced between cases whose `rebalance_weeks` differ, with a new reason, `different_rebalance`, and both values are shown side by side: a weekly case's mean period return is over weeks, and a 4-weekly case's over 4 weeks. That resolves the [open question](#open-questions) otherwise than its default, which differenced them with a label; the default is the alternative. A value from the daily levels isn't differenced, with `different_period`, when the case's levels in its segment start or end on another date than the baseline's, as a split segment's can when the two results' coverage differs, though their daily series have the same dates.
22. **A value's dates are its inputs':** a value over a segment's levels gives the dates of its first and last levels, and one over its periods the earliest `Tran Dt` and the latest `End Dt` of those periods, never a declared date they don't have, so every value calculated from the levels keeps their `chart` dates ([D-48](../spec.md#decisions)). When the coverage is unavailable, the `full` segment's values from its periods give no dates, rather than the first and last `chart` dates that stand in for the coverage, since their periods' first and last dates aren't known. Only `coverage_share` and `spanning_period_count` give the segment's own first and last dates, which may be declared ones. The alternative, dating each value of a split segment by the segment, would date a return through a Friday by the Sunday a split was declared on.
23. **The `inputs` of a value without a subject** name the series whose dates it reads: `["period"]` on `period_count` and `spanning_period_count`, and `["daily"]` on the windows' two counts; `coverage_share`, from the coverage and the declared dates alone, has `[]`. The alternative, naming every subject of the series, such as `["daily/strategy", "daily/benchmark"]`, would keep a count from being differenced between cases whose benchmarks differ, though no count depends on the benchmark.
24. **A coverage of one date has no share.** When the coverage's first and last dates are the same, as for a result without period rows whose `chart` has one date, each available split segment's `coverage_share` is unavailable, with `not_applicable`, since there are no days to divide by; otherwise, a segment of one date has a `coverage_share` of 0. The alternative, a share of 0 or 100, would give a share of a test period that has no length.

## User outcome

Read, in the report of a run or an experiment, new or saved, each case's dated returns, a fixed set of descriptive analyses of them, each case's comparison with the baseline, and its equity and drawdown charts, without assembling a spreadsheet ([OUT-02 and OUT-03](../spec.md#users-and-outcomes)). This is the roadmap's example scenario, read in the CLI's report ([D-42](../spec.md#decisions), [release planning requirements](../roadmap.md#release-planning-requirements)).

**The research task.** Reproducing published factor research describes each variant's returns, not only its summary statistics: rolling returns over several holding periods, drawdowns, and the measures over the periods before and after the paper's sample and its publication ([primary use](../spec.md#primary-use), [METH-09.7, METH-09.9, and METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)). The researcher D-42 names needs each case's dated returns first, since every later statistic reads them: the roadmap's [statistical evaluation](../roadmap.md#after-030) target, 0.6.0, infers from the same series.

**The manual work it removes.** Today a report shows Portfolio123's summary statistics, and an experiment's report shows its cases' metrics side by side, without differences. The saved response holds each period's returns and each weekday's value of 100 invested, but Trial Folio doesn't interpret them ([METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)). A researcher who wants a drawdown's dates, rolling returns, period splits, a case's differences from the baseline, or a chart reads the responses into a spreadsheet, calculates each one, draws the charts, and records by hand which method they used, for every case of every experiment. With 0.5.0, the report gives them, by one fixed, versioned method, and so does a saved run's or experiment's report rendered again, offline, from its records.

## Included scope

- **Each case's dated series, as a normalized table.** The daily levels of 100 invested, for the strategy and the benchmark, from the response's `chart`; and each rebalance period's returns, for the strategy, the benchmark, and their difference, from its `results.rows` ([the verified layout](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1)). Each value keeps its date, its source location, its provenance, and the digits the response gave it. For a run, and for each case of an experiment, as it's normalized.
- **A fixed, versioned analysis set.** Its first version, analysis set 1.0.0, is calculated from those series alone, identically for every case and every saved run or experiment ([METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)):
  - full-period summaries, the descriptive t-value of mean excess return per rebalance period among them, labeled descriptive with its number of periods ([METH-04.6](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge));
  - drawdowns, from the daily levels;
  - rolling returns over several holding periods ([METH-09.7](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research));
  - period splits, at dates the configuration declares before the run ([METH-09.9](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)).

  Where it recalculates a metric Portfolio123 reports, such as total return or maximum drawdown, the report shows both. R05-T02 specifies every calculation ([open questions](#open-questions)).
- **The explicit baseline comparison,** in an experiment's report: each case beside the baseline, with the difference of each metric and each analysis value ([D-42](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **Equity and drawdown charts,** drawn from the daily levels, as inline SVG in the self-contained report, which stays free of scripts ([REQ-08](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [D-42](../spec.md#decisions)): each case's, with its benchmark, and in an experiment the baseline's beside it.
- **Saved runs and experiments, rendered again.** `trialfolio report` renders a saved experiment's report again, offline, as it already does a saved run's, so the runs and experiments that releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote get the analytics from their saved responses, without a request ([INV-05](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)). It writes nothing into them ([ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).

## Explicit exclusions

- **Inference.** Confidence intervals, p-values, significance tests and grades, multiple-testing adjustment, the bootstrap, the deflated Sharpe ratio, and the probability of backtest overfitting are the [statistical evaluation](../roadmap.md#after-030) target's, after its methods ADR ([METH-04](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge)). The t-value is descriptive, and is never called a test.
- **Measures that need a risk-free series,** such as a Sharpe or Sortino ratio Trial Folio calculates, since no response holds one ([METH-01.3](../methodology.md#meth-01-define-the-question-before-measuring-success)). The report still shows Portfolio123's own, as reported.
- **Analyses that need new provider data:** repeated backtests from other entry dates, rolling screens, quantile buckets, and simulations ([METH-09.7 and METH-09.8](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research), [descriptive analysis before further acquisition](../roadmap.md#descriptive-analysis-before-further-acquisition)). Gross versus net results, and cost sensitivity, which need verified cost semantics, stay in the reproduction and sensitivity workflow ([METH-06.6](../methodology.md#meth-06-robustness-and-economic-usefulness)).
- **Ordering, ranking, or selecting cases by a value,** a "best" case, and any aggregate score ([METH-07](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
- **A review's report,** which keeps 0.4.0's: an experiment's report gives the comparison with the baseline. Comparing separate runs by their series, such as the same screen on two universes ([D-19](../spec.md#decisions)), and comparing the cases of two experiments, wait for a later release.
- **A graphical application, interactive charts, and any script,** which stay in the results workspace ([REQ-08](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [D-42](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **Study records:** protocols, deviation records, and amendments, which 0.6.0 and the reproduction and sensitivity workflow define.
- **Any change to what Portfolio123 reported.** `metrics.csv` and its values stay as 0.1.0 reads them.
- **`trialfolio report` writing into what it reads.** It writes only into its own output directory, and the saved run or experiment keeps its files ([ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).
- **Ranking snapshots,** which hold no series.
- **Parallel or scheduled execution,** as in 0.4.0.

## Required behavior

1. **Only what the response holds.** Every series comes from a case's saved response. A case whose response lacks one says so, with the reason, and nothing is reconstructed from summary statistics, filled with zero, or charted without its series ([METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim), [INV-03](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)).
2. **Offline and repeatable.** The analyses and charts come from archived artifacts, with no provider request. The same records, rendered with the same version, give the same bytes. `trialfolio report` writes nothing into the run or experiment it reads, and no command changes a file a run or experiment already holds ([INV-05](../spec.md#enduring-invariants), [ADR 0003](../adrs/0003-versioned-research-artifacts.md)).
3. **One fixed method.** The analysis set's version is recorded with every value it gives, and applies identically to every case and every saved run or experiment. Changing a calculation makes a new version ([METH-09.10](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research)).
4. **Calculated values say how.** Each is labeled calculated, with its method, the analysis set's version, its inputs, and the Trial Folio version, and is never given more digits than its inputs justify ([INV-06](../spec.md#enduring-invariants), [METH-05.10](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)).
5. **Reported values stay as reported.** Where the analysis set recalculates a metric Portfolio123 reported, the report shows both, rather than choosing one ([INV-08](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)), and flags a difference only beyond the range that the rounding of the recalculated value's inputs, and of the reported value, allows ([open questions](#open-questions)).
6. **Descriptive, and labeled so.** Each summary of overlapping windows says they aren't independent, with its number of windows and of non-overlapping windows. The t-value is labeled descriptive, with its number of periods. Statistical validation and trading readiness read "Not assessed" ([METH-04.6](../methodology.md#meth-04-inference-before-any-statistical-badge), [METH-09.7](../methodology.md#meth-09-reproducing-published-research), [METH-07.3](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
7. **A neutral comparison.** The baseline comparison and the charts order, rank, and mark no case by a value, and name no case best ([REQ-13](../spec.md#enduring-requirements), [METH-07](../methodology.md#meth-07-multidimensional-assessment-and-permitted-conclusions)).
8. **Everything 0.4.0 requires still holds,** and every artifact releases 0.1.0 to 0.4.0 wrote stays readable ([INV-12](../spec.md#enduring-invariants)).

## Failure and incomplete-data behavior

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| A case's response has no `chart`, or its arrays aren't parallel, dated, or numbers | That case's daily series is unavailable, with the reason. Its drawdowns, rolling returns, and charts are unavailable, and the other cases are still analyzed. |
| A case's `chart` isn't one entry per weekday from its start date to its end date, as the [verified layout](../contracts.md#p123api-screen-backtest-version-1) records it | That case's daily series is unavailable, with the reason, as for a `chart` that can't be read. Its drawdowns, rolling returns, and charts are unavailable. Its period series is still analyzed, and so are the other cases. |
| A case's response has no period rows, or a row's return isn't a number | That case's period series, or that row's value, is unavailable with its reason, never zero. Each analysis that needs it is unavailable with `input_unavailable`. |
| Too few observations for a window or a statistic | That value is unavailable, with the count it had, and is never calculated from fewer |
| A recalculated value differs from Portfolio123's beyond the range that the rounding of its inputs, and of the reported value, allows | Both are shown, and the difference is flagged. A difference within that range isn't flagged. |
| A declared split date is outside a case's coverage | That split is flagged, and its values are unavailable where the coverage doesn't reach. No window is shortened to fit ([METH-05.4](../methodology.md#meth-05-data-integrity-appropriate-to-the-claim)). |
| A case's coverage differs from the baseline's | The comparison names the difference, and labels each value calculated over different dates |
| `trialfolio report` is given a directory that isn't a complete run or experiment | It fails, as for a run, with `input.not_a_run`, exit 3, and writes nothing. An experiment whose latest session has no manifest isn't complete ([reading a saved experiment](../contracts.md#reading-a-saved-experiment)). |
| An artifact has a schema version with no reader | `artifact.unknown_schema_version`, exit 3, as in 0.4.0 |
