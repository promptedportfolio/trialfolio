# Release 0.2.1: License version 1.1

**Status:** Ready. At the owner's direction on 2026-10-07 ([D-27](../spec.md#decisions)), this release was specified and built in one pull request, because it changes only the license, its identifier, and the version. The owner signed off on 2026-10-07, after deciding its open question, and approved its merge. It is tagged `v0.2.1` on the commit that merges it. The owner sets its later statuses.
**Depends on:** [0.2.0](0.2.0-review.md)
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## User outcome

Individual investors who hold their investments through their own revocable trust, a retirement account, or a single-member LLC can use Trial Folio under the personal research grant without asking for permission, because version 1.1 of the license says so plainly ([ADR 0008](../adrs/0008-license-version-1-1.md)).

## Included scope

- LICENSE version 1.1, `LicenseRef-NSPRL-1.1`, as [ADR 0008](../adrs/0008-license-version-1-1.md) decides: the Personal Vehicle definition (section 1.21) and the sections that use it (1.6, 1.7, 1.8, 1.10, and 4), without the sentence about adoption that version 1.0 kept from its draft.
- The identifier that every artifact, report, and acknowledgment gives, and that the package's metadata declares; and the package's version, 0.2.1.
- The documents that describe the license, and the [written permission template](../written-permission-template.md) ([D-28](../spec.md#decisions)).

## Explicit exclusions

- No change to any command's behavior, any artifact schema, or the research notice. The notice's text is unchanged, so its version stays 1.0.
- No change to what releases 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 carry. Their tags, `v0.1.0` and `v0.2.0`, keep version 1.0 of the license, and copies received under it keep its terms (LICENSE section 19; [LIC-20](../licensing-policy.md#lic-20-license-changes-and-earlier-grants)).
- No distribution. Like 0.1.0 and 0.2.0, 0.2.1 is Released only when it's distributed from the brand's site ([D-22](../spec.md#decisions)).

## Required behavior

- **The identifier.** Every artifact that records a `license_id` records `LicenseRef-NSPRL-1.1`. Every report states it, with notice version 1.0, and links to the LICENSE at `v0.2.1` ([D-21](../spec.md#decisions)).
- **Acknowledgment.** An acknowledgment covers one license identifier and one notice version ([contracts.md, license acknowledgment](../contracts.md#license-acknowledgment)). So a record of `LicenseRef-NSPRL-1.0` doesn't cover 1.1: `run`, `report`, `demo`, and `review` ask again, and `TRIALFOLIO_ACCEPT_LICENSE` takes `LicenseRef-NSPRL-1.1/1.0`.
- **Saved runs and reviews.** Runs and reviews written under 1.0 keep their `license_id`, and still read, report, and review, because a `license_id` is checked only for its form. A report that 0.2.1 renders again from such a run states 1.1 and links to `v0.2.1`'s LICENSE, because a report carries the notices and license of the version that renders it.
- **The package.** Its version is 0.2.1, its metadata declares `LicenseRef-NSPRL-1.1`, and it carries the 1.1 LICENSE.

## Open questions

None remain.

- **A Family Member's own vehicles.** Resolved by the owner on 2026-10-07: not included. A Personal Vehicle is only the individual's own revocable trust, retirement account, or single-member LLC, not a Family Member's, such as a spouse's IRA that the individual manages. Anyone unsure whether such use is covered can ask for written permission ([CONTACT.md](../../CONTACT.md#permission-requests)).
