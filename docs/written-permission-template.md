# Written permission template

Nathan Slaughter uses this form to grant Written Permission under [LICENSE](../LICENSE) section 10 ([D-28](spec.md#decisions)). It's public so that anyone asking for permission can see what a grant contains.

**This template grants nothing.** A grant exists only when Nathan Slaughter sends it from git@nathanslaughter.com, or signs it, with its fields filled in (LICENSE section 10.1). A copy that anyone else fills in is not permission. [CONTACT.md](../CONTACT.md#permission-requests) says how to ask.

## Filling it in

- Fill in every bracketed field, and delete the choices that don't apply.
- Name the recipient exactly. For an organization, give its legal name, its type, and where it's organized.
- State the scope as narrowly as the use needs. A grant covers only what it states (section 10.3).
- Write "None" in item 7 unless publication, distribution, sublicensing, or a hosted service is granted. Permission to use Trial Folio doesn't include them unless the grant says so (section 5.3).
- Keep fees, support, and other commercial terms in a separate agreement, and name it in item 8. That agreement doesn't enlarge the grant.
- Keep a copy of every grant sent, under its number.

## Common requests

| Situation | Item 2 | Usual scope |
|---|---|---|
| An individual with more than US $5,000,000 in liquid assets, researching only their own and their family's investments | Section 1.7, condition (a) | The recipient only; their own research; item 7 "None" |
| An advisor or manager researching for clients | Section 1.7, condition (b) | The recipient, and any staff named in item 3; research for the recipient's clients |
| A company, fund, partnership, family office, or other organization | Section 4 | The users named in item 3; the organization's own research |
| Publishing or distributing code, a package, or an image, or running a hosted service | Section 5 | Item 7 states each right |

```text
Date:          [YYYY-MM-DD]
From:          Nathan Slaughter, the Licensor, git@nathanslaughter.com
Recipient:     [Full legal name of the person or organization]
               [For an organization: its type and where it is organized]
               [Contact address]

1. License. This is Written Permission under section 10 of the Nathan
   Slaughter Personal Research License, version [1.1]
   (LicenseRef-NSPRL-[1.1]). That license governs everything this grant
   does not address.

2. Why permission is needed. [Mark each that applies.]
   [ ] The recipient is a Professional User under section 1.7,
       condition [(a) liquid assets | (b) investment decisions for others |
       (c) reselling research].
   [ ] The use is for an Organization, or on an Organization's behalf
       (section 4).
   [ ] The use includes a use that section 5 reserves: [describe it].
   [ ] Other: [describe it].

3. Permitted users. [The recipient only | The recipient and up to [N] of
   its employees and contractors, while working for it | These named
   individuals: [names]]

4. Permitted purpose. [For example: research for the recipient's own
   investment decisions | research for the recipient's clients |
   evaluation for [N] days]

5. Versions. [Trial Folio [X.Y.Z] | Trial Folio [X.Y.Z] through [X.Y.Z] |
   every version released during the term in item 6]

6. Duration. [From the date above until [YYYY-MM-DD] | For the term of
   the agreement in item 8 | Until the Licensor revokes it in writing,
   with [N] days' notice]

7. Publication, distribution, sublicensing, and hosted services.
   [None. This grant permits none of the uses section 5 reserves.]
   [Or state each right expressly: what may be published or distributed,
   where, to whom, in what form, whether modified, and how the license
   and notices will travel with it.]

8. Related agreement. [None | [The agreement's name and date], which
   holds any fees, support, and other commercial terms, and does not
   enlarge this grant.]

9. Conditions.
   (a) Copies and Modifications stay on devices, accounts, and storage
       that only the permitted users can access, unless item 7 says
       otherwise.
   (b) The notices section 9.1 lists stay intact in every copy and
       Modification, and so do the notices in Reports.
   (c) This grant is personal to the recipient. It cannot be transferred
       or sublicensed unless item 7 says so.
   (d) This grant ends automatically if the recipient breaches it or the
       license (section 12).
   (e) This grant gives no right to Portfolio123's data or services,
       which Portfolio123's terms govern.
   (f) [Any other condition, or "None".]

Nathan Slaughter                                      Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
[Signature, or "Sent from git@nathanslaughter.com"]

Acknowledged by the recipient (optional): [Name]      Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
```
